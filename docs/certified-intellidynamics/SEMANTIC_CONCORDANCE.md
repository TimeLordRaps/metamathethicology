## Selected concrete interpretations

- The stable property is `effects/no-forbidden/v1`: no modeled persistent
  effect has its `forbidden` bit set.
- Environment and relational domains are finite enumerations carried by `V`
  and `R`. Claims about unlisted contexts or agents are outside the theorem.
- A certificate binds source and target configuration digests, policy version,
  checker, model, limit rule, authorization interpretation, invariant identity,
  authority, assumptions, and every context/peer case.
- `omega*k+n` is represented by Ordinatics `Ordinal((n, k))`. In the finite
  demonstration a natural-number encoding could also rank the states. The
  ordinal pair is useful because a decrease of `k` may reset `n`; no claim is
  made that finite machines intrinsically require transfinite ranks.
- Recovery disables ordinary, relational, proposal, installation, and authority
  mutation. One modeled environment step may occur between recovery steps. The
  scheduler must then take a recovery step. No real-time deadline is derived.
- Rollback restores only context, peer, and operational control state. It
  retains persistent effects and extends evidence. Irreversible effects are not
  represented as erased by checkpoint restoration.
